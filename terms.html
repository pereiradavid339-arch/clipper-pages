Terms of Service — Clipper

Last updated: October 2026

Clipper is a personal, single-user desktop tool, built and used only by its developer. It is not distributed, sold, or made available to other users.

Purpose

Clipper is a local desktop application that helps its developer edit and publish his own short-form videos to his own TikTok and YouTube accounts. It automates downloading source video, generating vertical clips, adding captions, and uploading the result to accounts the developer owns and controls.

Use of TikTok's platform

Clipper connects to TikTok solely through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API, under the developer's own authorization. It only ever posts to the TikTok account that authorized it, and only does so with the account holder's direct action.

No third-party access

No other person or account can use Clipper to publish content. It is not offered as a service, product, or tool to the public.

These terms may be updated as the app's functionality changes. Continued personal use after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.
