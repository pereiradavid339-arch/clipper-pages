Privacy Policy — Clipper

Last updated: October 2026

Clipper is a personal, single-user desktop tool. It is not a public product or service offered to other people — it is used only by its developer to manage his own TikTok and YouTube video uploads.

What this app does

Clipper runs locally on the developer's own computer. It downloads publicly available video content, generates short vertical video clips using local AI processing, and uploads the resulting clips to the developer's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

What data is accessed

  • The developer's own TikTok account, accessed via TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API, authorized directly by the developer.
  • The developer's own YouTube account, accessed via the YouTube Data API, authorized directly by the developer.
  • Publicly available video and metadata used as source material for clip generation.

What is not done

  • No data is collected from, or shared about, any third party or TikTok user.
  • No data is sold, rented, or shared with advertisers or other companies.
  • No account other than the developer's own is ever accessed.

Where data is stored

All processing happens locally on the developer's own computer. Access tokens obtained through TikTok's and YouTube's authorization flows are stored locally on that same computer and are never transmitted to any third-party server.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to the app's developer, David Pereira, at the email associated with the connected TikTok developer account.
